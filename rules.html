Game Rules – Looping Madness Party

Looping Madness Party is published by COSMOS AI SOLUTIONS SUARL (Tunisia), trading as Cosmos Games Studio, and sold on Steam.

1. Your license: the Steam Subscriber Agreement

You get the game through Steam, so the Steam Subscriber Agreement you accepted for your Steam account is the license for this game. It covers your right to play, purchases and refunds, what you may not do with the game (copying, reselling, cheating, hacking), Steam's conduct rules and account actions. The game has no separate license agreement to accept.

Looping Madness Party is for players aged 13 and older. If you are under the age of majority where you live, play with a parent's or guardian's permission.

These Game Rules add the few rules that are specific to Looping Madness Party. They don't replace or limit the Steam Subscriber Agreement, and nothing here takes away rights you have under the consumer-protection law of your country.

2. Your voice and your songs

  • What you record and the songs you make are yours, to the extent the law gives you rights in them. Recording your voice in the game doesn't transfer it to us, and the game never sends it to us.
  • Your recordings go only to the players in your party, and only if you allow it. Other players can keep a song with your voice only if you allow that too. See Voice Recording Permissions.
  • If you publish an exported song outside the game, you are responsible for having the rights and the permission of everyone whose voice is in it. See User-Generated Content Rules.

3. Playing with others

Be decent: no harassment, threats, hate, sexual content forced on others, or other people's personal information. Don't record other players with outside software to get around their choices. You can mute players, block or report them through their Steam profile, and the host can remove players from a party.

4. Items, points and scores

Cosmetic items in the game, and any points, achievements or leaderboard scores the game adds later, are part of playing the game.

  • They have no real-money value and can't be sold or exchanged for money through us. Only what Steam itself lets you trade can be traded, under Steam's rules.
  • We may correct points, scores or items gained by cheating or through a bug, and we may change how points are earned to keep the game fair and fun.

5. Fair play

No cheats, bots, modified game clients or attacks on other players' games or connections. Steam's own rules and enforcement apply to your Steam account.

6. Streaming

You may stream and make videos of the game, including on monetised channels. Respect the other players: tell your party you are live, and keep your chat moderated when it plays along in Streamer Mode. See User-Generated Content Rules (Streaming).

7. Updates

We may update the game, for example to fix bugs, keep multiplayer working or follow the law. Players need the same game version to play together. If these rules change in an important way, the game will tell you.
