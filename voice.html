Voice Recording Permissions

Your voice is the instrument in Looping Madness Party. These four switches decide what happens with it. You set them in the recording notice the first time you play, and change them any time in Settings → Privacy (or press M to mute).

1. Use my microphone

Off = the game never opens your microphone. You can still play: build songs from other players' clips and the built-in starter sounds.

When on, the mic opens only:

  • while your Loop-O-Matic screen is open,
  • during your own Stage-time turn,
  • during the 3-second mic test in Settings.

Whenever it is open you see ● MIC ON (bottom right, on every screen). Sound is only kept while you hold REC (R). The last few seconds are held in memory so a take can start exactly on the beat; they are overwritten all the time and wiped when the mic closes. No other player can open your mic.

2. Mute (M)

A quick switch: muted = the mic is closed everywhere until you unmute. Muting in the middle of a take stops it and throws it away.

3. Share my recordings with my party

Needed to record in a multiplayer round. When on, your Stage-time takes go into every party member's clip box for that round, they can use them in their songs, and your song is played to the party at the end of the round.

When off, you sit out Stage time (the game says so to the party) and can't record at the machine in a round, but you can still play with clips and starter sounds. Solo practice is not affected: it is offline.

Turning it off during a round works from that moment: your clips leave the other players' clip boxes and your voice is left out of later performances.

You can always press SKIP MY TURN on stage instead of recording.

4. Let other players keep songs with my voice

Off by default. When on, another player may save a song from the round that contains your voice in their MY SONGS, and export it as a WAV file. When off, the game leaves your voice out of their saved song and tells them why.

Taking part in a party never switches this on for you.

Technical limits (please read)

  • Turning a permission off works from now on. Audio already on another player's PC during a round stays in their memory until the round ends.
  • Songs someone saved or exported while you allowed it stay on their PC. The game cannot delete files on other people's computers.
  • Anyone can record their own speakers with other software. The game can't stop that, so only play with people you trust with your voice, and use mute, Steam block and leave when you need to.

Your own files

  • ★ kept clips: only your own takes; delete them in Settings → Privacy or by un-starring.
  • MY SONGS: your song projects; rename, delete or export them in MY SONGS.
  • Exports: WAV files in the export folder; delete them in Settings → Privacy or in the folder.

Nothing is uploaded by the game, and there is no public gallery.
