User-Generated Content Rules – Looping Madness Party

In Looping Madness Party you make music from recordings of your own voice. These rules cover those recordings, the songs you build, and party chat.

1. It's yours

  • You keep whatever rights the law gives you in your original recordings and songs.
  • Using your voice in the Game does not transfer ownership of your voice or recordings to COSMOS AI SOLUTIONS SUARL. We don't receive your recordings: the Game has no upload and no public gallery.
  • The built-in starter sounds (beats, claps, percussion, bass, chords) are made by us. You may use them for free in songs you export and in your videos, streams and releases, including monetised ones. You don't need to credit us, but we're happy if you do.

2. Respect other people's rights

  • Before you distribute a song outside the Game, make sure you have the rights you need. Singing or playing parts of existing songs (lyrics, melodies, recognisable recordings) can be protected by copyright, even in a funny remix.
  • The Game does not scan your recordings for copyright and does not block a recording because it sounds like an existing song. That's on purpose: what you do in your own party is up to you. Responsibility starts when you publish.

3. Respect other players' voices

  • A player's voice belongs to that player. You can only keep (save / export) a song containing another player's voice through the Game's save/export, and only if that player allowed it in their settings.
  • If a player changes their mind, respect it: don't publish their voice. The Game can't delete copies already exported, so ask before you post.
  • Don't record other players with outside software to get around their choices.

4. Not allowed

Recordings, songs, song names, party names and chat must not contain:

  • illegal content, or content that breaks someone else's rights;
  • harassment, threats, bullying, hate speech or discrimination;
  • sexual content involving minors (never, in any form), or sexual content forced on others;
  • content that encourages violence, self-harm or crime;
  • other people's personal information (doxxing), or impersonation.

5. What you can do about abuse

  • Mute a player (PLAYERS, in the lobby or the pause menu): you stop hearing their recordings, their songs and their chat.
  • Block or report a player on Steam: PLAYERS → STEAM PROFILE opens their Steam profile, where Steam's "Block" and "Report Player" options are. Blocked players are muted automatically and a host who blocked them won't let them in.
  • The host can remove a player from the party.
  • Leave the party at any time.

The Game has no moderation server of its own and stores no recordings online, so we can't review or remove recordings from players' PCs.

6. Streaming

  • When you stream the game, your audience sees and hears what you show, including other players' voices and songs in your party. Tell the other players you are live; anyone who doesn't want to be on stream can leave, mute you, or switch off sharing their recordings.
  • In Streamer Mode your chat can suggest words, vote and throw tomatoes and roses. Suggested words pass through Steam's text filter and a word it would change is dropped, but no filter is perfect: moderating your chat (for example with Twitch's AutoMod and your moderators) is your job as the streamer.
  • Streaming the game, including on monetised channels, is allowed. Your stream itself follows your streaming platform's rules.

7. Rights complaints and other concerns

If you believe something in the Game itself (its built-in content, store page or our official channels) infringes your rights, or you want to report a problem with these rules, contact anisd116@gmail.com with:

1. your name and contact details;

2. what the content is and where you found it;

3. which right you believe is infringed and why (for copyright: the work concerned);

4. a statement that the information is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them.

We acknowledge your message within 7 days and decide within 30 days. If we change or remove something because of a complaint, we tell the person affected where we can, and they can answer with their side of the story at the same address. The Game doesn't host or publish player content, so this address handles every rights and content question; there is no separate copyright agent or content-moderation contact.

For abuse by another player during a party, use Steam's reporting (section 5); we can't identify players or see what happened in a private party.

8. Changes

We may update these rules; the current version is always in the Game under Legal & Privacy.
